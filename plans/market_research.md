# Market research + competitors (2026-10-07)

## Market
- Note-taking / PKM: est. $7–13B (2026), ~20% CAGR. Estimates vary a lot. Crowded, AI is now table stakes
- Read-later gap: Pocket shut down Jul 2025. Users scattered to Readwise Reader ($9.99/mo), Matter, Instapaper, Raindrop
- Cute/gamified wellness works: Finch ~2.3M downloads per 90 days, 4.9★, ~76% female, aged 25–35, mostly US. Forest is used by tens of millions
- Real pain point: "99 ideas, 0 finished" is a common complaint (ADHD, multi-passionate creators, indie hackers). Current fixes are spreadsheets, "idea freezer" features, and weekly reviews

## Competitor map
No single app does capture → grow → inspire → project. Each competitor covers one part.

| Slice | Apps | What they do well | Gap Idea Garden fills |
|---|---|---|---|
| Notes / second brain | Notion, Obsidian, Apple Notes, Keep, Capacities ($9.99), Reflect ($10), Mem ($15), Fabric ($5) | Power, AI search, linking | Blank-page tools. Ideas get buried, nothing nudges them toward a project. Organizing them is a chore |
| Idea collectors | Napkin, mymind ($5–13), Einfall, Ikes | Fast capture, AI tags, daily resurfacing (Napkin) | Ideas never turn into projects. They only hold what *you* save |
| Idea → project apps | IDEA Pro, IdeaSparkKeeper, Mentor: Idea Studio, ADHD Pal (idea freezer) | Status, scoring, convert idea → tasks | Utilitarian lists, no emotional hook, no inspiration feed. Small, low-polish apps |
| Read-later / discovery | Readwise Reader, Matter, Feedly, Raindrop | Reading UX, highlights | Content isn't linked to your ideas. No "this paper fits your project" |
| Gamified growth | Finch, Forest, Habitica | Emotional attachment, retention, cute art | Gamifies focus or self-care, never ideas or creative work |
| Enterprise ideation | Aha!, Ideanote | Team funnels | Built for companies, not individuals |

## Closest threats
- **Napkin**: closest on "ideas plus resurfacing". It has no projects, no growth metaphor, and no external feed
- **Fabric / mymind**: calm, AI-organized saving. They could add "projects" easily
- **Notion AI / Capacities**: users already live there. "Good enough" is the real competitor
- **Finch-style indies**: if one makes "Finch for creative projects", it competes head-on

## Our key differentiators
1. **Whole idea lifecycle in one calm place**: seed → sprout → plant → bloom → project with milestones → learning goals. Others stop at capture or start at tasks
2. **Growth tied to real work**: an idea's stage = attention + links + inspirations + reflection. The garden *is* the progress report. Nobody else gamifies how ideas mature
3. **Inspiration comes to you, matched to your ideas**: 51 credible feeds + OpenAlex papers, with taste learning and "for your project" picks. Competitors only store what you save, and read-later apps don't know your projects
4. **Anti-overwhelm by design**: 1 main project + side projects, ≤3 contextual suggestions, terrarium for parking ideas. Aimed at people with too many ideas, not too few
5. **Emotional hook**: cute garden/island art, like Finch's retention mechanism, aimed at creative work
6. **Low friction**: free, web, no install, local-first, guest mode, Google sync

| No monetization, solo dev | — | see below |
| Discover feed is generic (RSS) | Feedly, Reader | Fine as long as matching to ideas stays the selling point |

## How to compete
- **Don't fight Notion/Obsidian on power.** Position as the *incubator* that sits upstream of them: grow here, export finished plans there
- **Positioning line:** "A calm garden where your ideas grow into finished projects", or as shorthand: "Finch for your ideas"
- **Target niche first:** multi-passionate creatives, students, indie hackers, ADHD adults (25–35, Finch's demographic overlaps)
- **Retention ritual:** weekly "garden walk" (resurface wilting ideas, pick one to water). Napkin's daily resurfacing proves people want this
- **Channels:** r/ADHD, r/SideProject, Indie Hackers, Product Hunt launch after P8, TikTok/IG (cute garden screenshots are shareable)
- **Monetization (Finch model):** core free. Paid tier (~$4–6/mo) for real AI (think/plan), 3D garden + cosmetic decor (P7), unlimited projects/history. Undercuts Reflect/Mem/Readwise

## Priority implications
1. P8 mobile capture before more features. It's the biggest gap vs every competitor
2. Finish P6 AI and the embeddings-based "for your project" matching. That's the moat
3. P7 3D garden plus cosmetics = monetization + shareability
4. Add Markdown export (cheap, removes an objection)
